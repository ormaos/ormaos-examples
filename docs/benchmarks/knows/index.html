Why knows2 replaced it

In knows, the programs, the executions and the questions were all written by the people who make Ormaos, and the questions ask about calls, exceptions, shapes and I/O: what Ormaos records. That choice of questions can favour the recording. knows2 keeps the same executions and an oracle under sys.settrace, and has the questions written by a model that saw only the source, the README and the printed output of each program and was not told about Ormaos; it adds a fourth arm, a recording with values, counts every answer as right, null or wrong, and drops a question only when the oracle cannot answer it. The tasks and the oracle are described on its page.

Limits

As the run's report lists them:

  • No expectation was written before this run: the rules of rules.json were, what each arm would score was not.
  • In 4 of 9 tasks every arm scored 1.00 (web-backend, ml-training, rl-env, freight-quote); those tasks do not separate the arms.
  • Of the gap of 0.06 between raw and ormaos in the mean score, 0.05 comes from data-pipeline: its questions ask the row counts of DataFrames, which Ormaos records and VizTracer with its default settings does not.
